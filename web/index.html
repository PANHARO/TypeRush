A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Make yourself comfortable.

Find your flow

Type the highlighted word, then press Space or Enter. Your first letter starts the clock. Backspace fixes the current word; submitted words are final.

Two ways to practice

Speed Test: choose 15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds.
Survival: start with 15 seconds. A correct word adds 2 seconds; a wrong word removes 1 second. The timer holds up to 30 seconds.

Every character counts

WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes played. Accuracy = correct characters ÷ typed characters × 100. Both include your current input. A submitted space counts as correct only after a correct word. Corrected letters replace mistakes.

Only completed rounds save scores, and only when signed in to a local profile. The top 10 results are kept per Speed Test duration and for Survival time. The clock keeps running while dialogs are open or the app is in the background.

Keep your scores safe

Normal updates preserve scores. Deleting app data can remove local profiles and scores. Export backups regularly; they contain scores, not your PIN. Local profiles do not sync between devices.

YOUR NEXT ROUND, ONE TAP AWAY

Keep TypeRush close.

Install from your browser. No app store needed. Once “Offline ready” appears, both modes work without a connection.

  1. iPhone & iPadOpen this site in Safari. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Enable “Open as Web App” if shown, then tap Add.
  2. AndroidOpen in Chrome. Open the browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. WindowsOpen in Chrome or Edge. Use the install icon in the address bar, or the browser’s app-install menu.
  4. MacUse Safari’s File → Add to Dock on macOS Sonoma or later, or install from Chrome’s menu.

On a phone, turn off predictive text and autocorrect in your keyboard settings if they interfere with practice.
